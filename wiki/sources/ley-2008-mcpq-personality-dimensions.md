# Source: Разработка MCPQ — пять измерений personality companion dogs (Ley et al. 2008)

**Оригинал:** [`статьи/foreign/australia/Ley_2008_MCPQ_personality_dimensions_companion_canines.pdf`](../../статьи/foreign/australia/Ley_2008_MCPQ_personality_dimensions_companion_canines.pdf)

Ley, J., Bennett, P., & Coleman, G. (2008). Personality dimensions that emerge in companion canines. *Applied Animal Behaviour Science, 110*(3–4), 305–317. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applanim.2007.04.016

## Саммари

### Суть

Многоэтапная разработка **Monash Canine Personality Questionnaire (MCPQ)** по psycholexical methodology (аналог Big Five у людей): focus groups → pilot reduction → PCA на >1000 owners. Итог — **5 factors**, **41 adjective** для rating знакомой собаки.

### Этапы и выборка

| Этап | N / материал | Результат |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| Study 1: word generation | 2 focus groups × 6 | **203** adjectives |
| Study 2: word reduction | 92 owners (pilot) | **67** adjectives |
| Study 3: PCA | **1016** Australian owners (online/media) | **5 factors**, 41 words retained |

Критерии: собака ≥18 months; owner ≥18 years. Congruence coefficients между M/F subsamples ≥0.95.

### Пять измерений MCPQ

| Factor | % variance | Cronbach α | Label (tentative) | Content |
|--------|------------|------------|-------------------|---------|
| 1 | 8.3% | 0.901 | **Extraversion** | energetic, active, excitable, restless… |
| 2 | 6.7% | 0.831 | **Training focus** | obedient, trainable, attentive, biddable… |
| 3 | 6.5% | 0.814 | **Self-assuredness/Motivation** | determined, assertive, tenacious, dominant… |
| 4 | 6.4% | 0.845 | **Amicability** | friendly, sociable, non-aggressive, relaxed… |
| 5 | 4.6% | 0.798 | **Neuroticism** | fearful, nervous, timid, cautious… |

**Total variance explained (final 41 items):** 32.6%. Extraversion и neuroticism — partial homology с human/other species models; training focus, amicability, self-assuredness/motivation — возможно **canine-specific** (selective pressure от humans).

### Ограничения

- Convenience sample (media/internet); Australian companion dogs only.
- Positive bias owners → low-variance words removed (может недооценить neuroticism spread).
- «Openness to experience» не выделился отдельно.
- Validation/reliability на follow-up (Ley 2009).

### Релевантность для Barko

- **K3:** MCPQ dimensions map loosely на C-BARQ constructs (excitability ↔ extraversion; trainability ↔ training focus; fear ↔ neuroticism) — потенциал **cross-instrument harmonization**, не готовые features без mapping study.
- **K5:** reference для personality lexicon, shelter matching, «energy vs trainability vs amicability» в owner education; precursor MCPQ-R (26 items).
